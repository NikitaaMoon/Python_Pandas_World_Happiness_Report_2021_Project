## 📌 PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project analyzes the **World Happiness Report 2021** dataset to uncover the key factors influencing happiness scores across different countries.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns, trends, and relationships between happiness and socioeconomic indicators such as:

- GDP per Capita
- Social Support
- Healthy Life Expectancy
- Freedom to Make Life Choices
- Generosity
- Perceptions of Corruption

The project provides meaningful visualizations and data-driven insights to better understand global well-being.

---

## 🎯 PROBLEM SOLVED

Organizations, researchers, and policymakers often struggle to understand which factors contribute most to a country's happiness and quality of life.

This project helps to:

✔ Identify key drivers of happiness

✔ Compare country-wise happiness performance

✔ Support evidence-based decision-making

✔ Visualize complex relationships through interactive analytics

## 📈 KEY RESULTS

### Insights Discovered

- Countries with higher GDP per capita generally report higher happiness scores.
- Social support emerged as one of the strongest predictors of happiness.
- Healthy life expectancy positively impacts overall well-being.
- Freedom of choice contributes significantly to happiness rankings.
- Regional differences reveal varying happiness determinants.

### Analysis Outcomes

| Metric | Observation |
|----------|------------|
| Top Happiness Countries | Highest overall happiness scores |
| GDP Correlation | Strong Positive Relationship |
| Social Support Impact | High Influence |
| Life Expectancy Impact | Significant Contributor |
| Freedom Score Impact | Positive Correlation |
